Our story

Honest is a recipe.

Purely You started at an Owasso farmers market table with one banana bread and a strong opinion: treats shouldn't need a confession afterward. Three years later the opinion hasn't changed — the ovens just got bigger.

  • No. 1Ingredients you can pronounceDates, oats, honey, almond flour. The label reads like a pantry, not a lab.
  • No. 2Small batch, every batchIf it didn't come out right, it doesn't go in the case. Ask about "oops bags" on Fridays.
  • No. 3Everyone eats, dogs includedGluten-free, dairy-light, and a whole case just for the pups tied up out front.
