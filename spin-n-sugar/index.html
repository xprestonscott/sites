      Who we are

      A mother, a daughter, and one very loud machine

      Spin N Sugar is a home business in Glenpool, Oklahoma. We started spinning at local events and it grew the way good things do around here — one birthday, one church festival, one wedding at a time.

      What makes ours different is the sugar itself: our pastry chef makes every cotton candy flavor we spin, from the everyday pink vanilla to the whole fall board. That's why you'll find things here you won't find at a fair — caramel apple cider, sweet potato pie, cinnamon roll icing.

      We live spin at events for hire, and we sell fresh cotton candy by the tub, the dome cup and the 12 inch cake. Pickup here in Glenpool, shipping if you're further out.
