Step 01 — Inspect

The full-perimeter walk

Attic, crawlspace, foundation, and yard. We map every entry point and conducive condition before a drop is sprayed.

  • Moisture & harborage check
  • Entry-point mapping
  • Written findings, photographed
Step 02 — Treat

Precision application

Products matched to the pest and the household — applied to cracks, voids, and harborage zones, not fogged over everything you own.

  • Kid & pet safe selections
  • Interior + exterior barrier
  • Targeted, not blanket
Step 03 — Seal

Exclusion work

Copper mesh, sealant, and door sweeps on every gap we found in step one. Prevention beats re-treatment, every time.

  • Gaps & weep holes closed
  • Pipe chases meshed
  • Vents screened
Step 04 — Stand by it

The written guarantee

If activity returns between scheduled visits, we come back at no charge. That promise is printed on your invoice.

  • Free re-treats between visits
  • Text-ahead arrival windows
