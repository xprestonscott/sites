Fair questions

What you're probably thinking

I already have a website.

Most guys I talk to do. That's fine — the website isn't the point. The point is what happens when someone calls you and you're forty feet up. Your current site doesn't text anybody back. I can keep your site and put the system on it, or rebuild it. Your call.

Why is there a monthly fee?

Because there's a monthly service. It's carrying your phone number, sending texts, hosting the site, and me making whatever changes you want. If it were just a file sitting on a server I'd charge you once and disappear — but then it wouldn't do anything for you.

Is this one of those AI chatbots?

No. It's a text message from your number that says you missed them and asks what they need. When they write back, you answer — it's your customer and your conversation. Nothing pretends to be you.

What if it doesn't get me anything?

Then you'd see that on the dashboard and you'd cancel, and you should. That's exactly why the dashboard exists. I'd rather you be able to check than have to trust me.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Month to month, 30 days' notice. The website is yours either way — I'll hand over the files. The phone automation stops when you stop paying for it, because it costs money to run.

I'm too busy for this.

Setup takes about twenty minutes of your time total — I need your logo, some job photos, and a five-minute call about what you do. Everything else is on me.

Let's find out what you're missing.

Fifteen minutes on the phone. I'll ask what a job's worth to you and roughly how many calls get away — and you'll know the number by the end of the call. No pitch deck.
